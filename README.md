# properties-dev.com

Official repository for **properties-dev.com** — *Independent Real Estate Consultancy & Strategic Marketing Partner*.

## Overview

A modern, ultra-luxurious, and mobile-first corporate homepage for **properties-dev.com**, curated under a **Calm Luxury** aesthetic with strict **Zero Gold** compliance.

### Core Architecture & Features

- **Strict Zero Gold Compliance**: Absolutely no gold, bronze, brass, or yellow tones anywhere in the design.
- **Calm Luxury Palette**: Crisp whites (`#FFFFFF`), alabaster (`#FAFBFC`), and subtle stone grey (`#F8F9FA` / `#F1F3F5`) combined with deep rich charcoal/slate (`#1A202C` / `#2D3748`) and frosted glass containers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`).
- **Hero Section (Top: 0)**: Starts directly at the top of the viewport with no fixed top navbar, featuring top-center branding `PROPERTIES-DEV` and sub-branding `By properties-dev.com`.
- **Lead Capture Form**: Frosted glass container with blank inputs (no placeholder text), split international phone input with clean UTF-8 flag emojis (defaulting to Egypt `+20`), solid dark charcoal submit button, and regulatory disclaimer.
- **Our Expertise & Market Leadership**: Decades of trusted market leadership highlighting extensive experience, 100% unbiased advisory, and exclusive developer access.
- **Nationwide Destinations Portfolio**: High-resolution destination showcases for *New Cairo & Mostakbal City*, *North Coast (Ras El Hekma)*, *West Cairo*, and *Red Sea Destinations*.
- **Direct WhatsApp Integration**: Solid WhatsApp Green buttons (`#25D366`) and fixed bottom-right floating icon linking to `+201033373331`.
- **Standalone Routed Legal Pages**:
  - `Privacy Policy` (`/privacy-policy` and `privacy-policy.html`)
  - `Terms & Conditions` (`/terms-conditions` and `terms-conditions.html`)
  - Styled with 100% white text on solid black (`#000000`), explicitly displaying company entity `properties-dev.com`, stating data collection policy, and under the exclusive jurisdiction of the **Courts of Cairo, Arab Republic of Egypt**.

## Local Development & Preview

Run the lightweight preview server using Node.js:

```bash
node server.js
```

Or open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser.
